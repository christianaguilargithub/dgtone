Who we are

Long-term relationships, plain-spoken care.

Founded and managed by Maria Theresa G. Tamayo, DMD and Raffy D.G. Tamayo, DMD, DGT One has a team of multiskilled, compassionate dental professionals across Metro Manila and nearby provinces.
